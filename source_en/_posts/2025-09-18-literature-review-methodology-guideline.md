<h2>Systemical overview report</h2>
<p>The systematic overview was initially developed mainly in the field of medical sciences, with the aim of integrating findings in a systematic, transparent and replicable manner and was recognized as part of the synthesis methodology&quot;Gold Standard&quot;。</p>
<p>Systematic synthesis can be defined as a research methodology and process for identifying and critically assessing relevant studies and collecting and analysing data from these studies. <p>Systemic freedom review <strong>meta-analysis</strong> The results of the study are being consolidated. <h2>Integrated review</h2>
<p>Closely related to semi-structured overviews are integrated overviews (or critical overviews) approaches. <p>For mature topics, the integrated synthesis approach aims to provide a comprehensive overview of the knowledge base, critically examining and possibly re-conceptively broadening its theoretical base as the topic develops. <p>The integrated synthesis approach should aim to advance the evolution of knowledge progress and theoretical frameworks, not just an overview or description of a particular research area. <h2>Points and common error zones</h2>
<p>While there are many benefits to the literature synthesis, its successful publication is challenging. Many researchers have made common errors in their literature overviews, which have made it difficult for them to enter the deliberative landscape of quality journals.</p>
<p>** First, the synthesis of the literature was not sufficiently detailed. ** This prevented reviewers from assessing the quality of the synthesis and its academic contributions. Such overviews often lack a description of the overall research strategy and the specific operational details of the analytical methodology.</p>
<p>** Second, in order to streamline the workload, researchers are often anxious to narrow down the sample of literature. ** This unduly limits the scope of the search. Of course, it is reasonable sometimes to properly limit the sample, but clear and strong reasons must be provided to support this option.</p>
<p>** Third, many researchers have not been able to present and explain their findings clearly after completing their synthesis. ** They often contain a large number of charts, tables and graphics without explanation or comment, making it difficult for readers to understand the actual meaning or true discovery of these visualized content.</p>
<p>** Fourth, perhaps the most common error: the literature review has failed to really bring valuable and innovative contributions to the field. ** No matter how rigorous and well structured an overview is, its failure to provide any new, substantive information is bound not to be published.</p>
