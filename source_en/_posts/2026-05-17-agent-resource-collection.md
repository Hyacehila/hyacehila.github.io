<h2>Something that was invented by independent developers.</h2>
<h3><a href="https://github.com/Hyacehila/General-Research-Review-Agent-And-Skills">General Research Review Agent And Skills</a></h3>
<p><strong>Type: Skill</strong></p>
<p>This is a set of research reports that I made, mainly to assist with general writing. It's not a single line of "help me write an overview" but it's a few steps to break the scooping review down: finding literature, weighting, screening candidate pools, extracting paper evidence, organizing outlines, consolidating the text, and finally checking references and language.</p>
<p>My favorite thing is that it turns the synthesis from a chat to a process with intermediate products. In the past, such work was often done by Zotero, Google Scholar, manual forms, scattered PDF, temporary prompts, and finally LLM to help with the tanning. It can certainly be done, but it's painful to look back: which articles are excluded, why, which ones are supported by, and whether or not the quotations are mistook.</p>
<p>This skills handles these places. The final PDF/HTML/Markdown report will be left. It is not about making the article more beautiful, but about making the most easily dispersed part of the overview available: where it comes from, where it filters, where it is written, what it supports. It is more suitable for a broad-based, audit-required study than an ad hoc prompt or simple documentation management tool; if only a few papers were summed up, there would be no need for such a process.</p>
